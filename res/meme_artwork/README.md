# Meme tile artwork

Generated for Tile World Online and DATTools with the built-in OpenAI image
-generation tool on 2026-10-20. The transparent PNG is the original generated
asset; both apps scale the atlas frames with nearest-neighbor sampling at runtime.
DATTools publishes identical `meme.png` and `meme.json` bytes.

Art brief: a transparent retro pixel-art atlas matching the existing Expanded
sprites, with dark outlines and clear silhouettes at 48 px. Row 1: brown Hint
boots with a large yellow question mark; Red tank facing north, west, south.
Row 2: Red tank facing east; red button with a white clockwise curved arrow;
red button with the mirrored counterclockwise arrow; unused transparent cell.
No labels, backgrounds, watermarks, or extra sprites.

`meme.json` records integer frame bounds for the generated 1774×887 image.
DAT codes are 0x76 Hint boots, 0x77–0x7A Red tank N/W/S/E, 0x7B clockwise,
and 0x7C counterclockwise. The seven sprites represent four gameplay elements.
