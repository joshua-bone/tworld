# P7B training replay foundation

## Decision

The former immediate plan to finish the synthetic Phase-A curriculum and then
attempt one donor-blind real level is superseded. Synthetic levels remain fast
mechanics and failure-injection fixtures; they are not the training product.

The active sequence is:

1. process all of CCLP1 as training evidence;
2. process all of CCLP4 as training evidence;
3. reconcile and process all of official CCLP5, using voting-pack relatives
   where their exact relationship is proved; and
4. only then freeze the solver and evaluate it on a capability-isolated CCLP3.

This produces 447 real training levels and 894 target-ruleset records before
evaluation. P1B's original small curriculum remains immutable historical
evidence, but it no longer selects the next product milestone.

CCLP3 is a donor-withheld evaluation pack from the future freeze point, not a
claim of historical sight-unseen blindness. Its donor files exist in the
repository and some CCLP3 cases have already served as characterization or
design canaries. A contamination ledger must disclose those cases separately.

## Definition of a processed training target

A target record is processed only when it has all applicable results below:

- the exact source occurrence is rehashed, DATTools-valid, and accepted by the
  standard-CC1 source-scope gate;
- donor availability and provenance are explicit;
- every available raw donor entry remains immutable and is freshly replayed on
  its declared target;
- the uninterrupted replay is divided into ordered semantic segments whose
  intervals cover it and whose adjacent boundaries join;
- a portable replay candidate is generated when the evidence supports one;
- every variant-versus-execution-target claim has its own fresh certificate;
- transformations and remaining dependencies are recorded; and
- every published segment can be viewed through the shared replay player.

Terminal statuses include certified raw evidence, certified portable evidence,
target-specific behavior, required quirks, invalid replay, missing donor, and
categorized bounded failure. Missing data is part of the denominator, never a
silent exclusion.

## Immutable raw replay and portable candidate

Normalization never edits or replaces a donor replay. It creates a new,
lineage-bound candidate from an immutable raw replay and records an ordered
transformation ledger.

The first portable profile is `hybridcc-candidate-10hz-v1`, bound to the
HybridCC2026 timing and provisional replay-input contract at a declared engine
revision. Its authoritative timeline has zero-based 100 ms logic boundaries,
10 logic steps per second, and a two-step ordinary move. Sparse changes update
one held `(primary, secondary)` packet before a boundary transition. Release is
explicit; directions are cardinal; an ordered orthogonal secondary direction
retains Lynx-like fallback/slap intent.

Tile World Lynx remains a 20 Hz engine whose ordinary movement contains four
meaningful 50 ms native ticks. The profile does not relabel or skip those
ticks. Compiling one portable step to Lynx spans two native ticks, so one
ordinary move remains four native ticks while becoming two portable logic
steps. MS and Lynx replay certification therefore retain their independent
native clocks alongside the 10 Hz portable clock.

The profile may eliminate MS mouse goals, goal-preserving no-ops, redundant
polls, or fragile input carry only after a fresh execution proves the
replacement. Seed, stepping, initial random-slide direction, meaningful ordered
Lynx diagonal intent, waits that advance actors or randomness, forced-floor
timing, and device timing are never defaulted or mechanically discarded.

Evidence grades remain distinct:

- representation-lossless re-encoding;
- target-native exact equivalence;
- semantic equivalence with disclosed native timing/state differences;
- strategy-only lineage requiring target recompilation; and
- unproved or residual-quirk behavior.

HybridCC2026 currently has a private provisional slice replay and a documented
future native envelope, not a complete production adapter for these levels.
The CCSolver profile mirrors the stable semantic timing/input boundary through
a versioned file protocol; it does not copy or claim compatibility with that
private wire format. Until the adapter and production envelope exist, portable
results are `hybrid-candidate`, never Hybrid-certified.

## Segment player

The player keeps replay lineage and execution target as separate axes:

- variants: original MS, original Lynx, and portable;
- execution targets: MS and Lynx now, with a versioned Hybrid target only after
  that adapter exists.

The browser starts the complete replay from canonical initialization, seeks to
the selected segment through the genuine engine, retains only an in-memory
checkpoint/history, and plays the selected interval. It does not fabricate a
mid-level TWS.

Each level page provides a segment ribbon, play/pause, restart, single-tick
step, scrub, speed, semantic-anchor navigation, static starting/ending
evidence, and a provenance drawer. Unavailable or failed combinations remain
visible with a reason. The player does not autoplay, owns keyboard shortcuts
only while focused, and honors reduced motion.

At pack scale, pages share one player bundle and artwork atlases and lazy-load
compact level/replay data. They do not embed full frame histories or repeated
full-map SVGs in every page.

## Pack waves

### P7B — foundation and bounded real cohort

The bounded acceptance cohort is CCLP1 levels 001 through 010, plus level 042
as the nonzero-stepping canary and level 137 as the MS-mouse canary. It contains
12 real levels and 24 direct donor replays. The cohort proves the contracts,
bounded processor, exact browser transport, evidence closure, and shared
segment player needed by later pack waves. It is test evidence, not a separate
partial training-pack product: the first durable checked pack is the complete
149-level P7C CCLP1 pack.

### P7C — CCLP1 completion

Process all 149 levels and all 298 direct donor replays. Completion requires a
visible terminal status for every target, not merely a successful sample.

### P7D — CCLP4 completion

Process all 149 levels and all 298 direct donor replays through the same
pipeline. Pack-specific one-off dossier logic is not admitted.

### P7E — CCLP5 reconciliation and completion

Official CCLP5 contains 149 valid levels. Direct official coverage is 115
paired levels, one MS-only level, and 33 levels without a donor. Voting-pack
material is admitted under the following relationship policy:

1. Equal normalized-gameplay digest is an exact alias. Rebind the voting replay
   to the official occurrence and freshly verify it on that exact map.
2. Unequal gameplay digest is an edited relative, never an alias. A checked
   lineage record must bind both members and their deterministic map diff.
3. A verbatim replay that wins on the official map is a
   `transferred-verbatim` result, not an official donor.
4. A failed transfer records its first semantic divergence. Bounded repair may
   retain only a verified prefix; a repaired result is `adapted-relative` and
   receives a new full-run certificate.
5. MS and Lynx transfer status is independent. Title similarity alone conveys
   no replay authority.

The current exact-map audit finds 82 voting aliases. Fourteen backfill missing
official pairs, producing exact-map coverage of 129 paired levels, one MS-only
level, and 19 levels with neither target covered. Nineteen of the remaining 20
incomplete levels have a unique same-title edited voting relative; only
CCLP5/139, `Udassa`, has no same-title candidate. Candidate coverage is not
reported as certified until transfer or repair succeeds.

### P8 — sealed CCLP3 evaluation

After P7C through P7E, freeze solver code, tactics, normalization rules, budgets,
and evaluation policy. Run CCLP3 in a donor-redacted environment with explicit
capability denial, retain every attempt, and score known-contaminated cases
separately. Later donor comparison cannot rewrite the frozen attempt.

## P7B acceptance

- The exact 12-case cohort and 24 donors are pinned from checked corpus inputs.
- Raw replay bytes cannot be mutated through any normalization API.
- Segment intervals are bounded, contiguous, terminal-complete, and reconstruct
  their checked endpoints from canonical initialization.
- Variant and execution target cannot be conflated.
- CCLP1/137 either gains a freshly certified mouse-free MS candidate or records
  an exact residual failure.
- CCLP1/042 proves stepping is never silently defaulted.
- Portable timelines are canonical 10 Hz held-packet changes with explicit
  release; target compilation preserves and discloses the separate native
  clock.
- Meaningful ordered Lynx diagonal intent remains visible unless an
  independently certified replacement exists.
- The player lazy-loads one replay, never embeds frame histories, has no
  gameplay takeover, and passes keyboard/reduced-motion/browser checks.
- Generation is bounded and resumable; presentation attestation does not rerun
  engines.
