Why completion is impossible

  1. The initial player is at (14, 0), east of column x=5. Every one of the 32 top-layer cells (5, y), 0<=y<=31, is an ordinary Wall, with Empty below.
  2. The only Exit in either layer is (4, 0), west of that column. The level has no teleporters. Cardinal movement cannot leave or wrap around the 32 x 32 board.
  3. Ordinary walls cannot be removed by the available actors or buttons. Toggle buttons only exchange the toggle-wall tiles. Therefore no legal play can transfer Chip to the west side and reach the exit.
