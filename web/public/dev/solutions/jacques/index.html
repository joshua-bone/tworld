Dataset and method

The 986-level archive was compared with the author’s attachment; the level records match, while the attachment has thirteen additional empty placeholders. All results here refer to the exact archive hash below. No tiles or rules were changed.

Strategies and proofs were developed from full-map artwork, both tile layers, the MS implementation, and bounded experiments. Local timing searches are used where a specific collision or move order needs resolving. Replay verification uses the original C engine independently of the web implementation.
