Why completion is impossible

  1. All 434 chips are required and exactly 434 chip tiles exist; none can be omitted.
  2. 246 of these chips have a Beartrap immediately underneath. Example: chip at (1,1), bottom file-code43. No trap connections are encoded and no brown buttons, blocks or cloners exist.
  3. Collecting the chip pops its top tile and leaves Chip standing on Beartrap. The native/MS arrival path only releases such a buried trap when a trap connection targets it; none does.
  4. Subsequent movement rejects leaving Beartrap when Chip is not released. No available gameplay mechanism removes this trap or releases it. Roaming monsters cannot collect chips for the player, and collision is not a win.
  5. Since (1,1) must be collected, every purported winning route becomes permanently trapped there (or at another required trap chip) before reaching the distinct sole exit at (0,31). Therefore no winning input sequence exists.
