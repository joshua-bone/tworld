Findings so far

  1. The strongest preserved route is alive at (0,14), tick 2319, with 329 chips collected and 47 still required. Its initial state, all 2,320 steps and terminal result match original Tile World MS exactly. Only original cardinal keyboard inputs are used.
  2. The level contains 419 chip tiles but requires 376, so a solution may leave 43 chips uncollected. The current route leaves 90 chip tiles, of which at least 47 still need collecting.
  3. The route collects an upper-area prefix, escapes through (9,15), then clears most of the lower chip field. It acquires ice boots at (0,31), later obtains the red key and fire boots on the right edge, and uses the central fire enclosure as a refuge.
  4. After collecting the blue- and red-door room chips, it takes the yellow key at (0,20) and force boots at (0,14). The remaining water and green-key areas are still candidates for a different ordering; longer tool-collection detours let the automatic cloners fill more of the board.
  5. The red-ring strips in the upper area are clone-machine terrain, not teleporters. Those impassable strips restrict entry into the upper side compartments. Returning to the upper chips after clearing the lower board is the unresolved part of this route.
