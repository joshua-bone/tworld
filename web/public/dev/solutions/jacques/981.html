Winning strategy

  1. Take the reusable green key at (16,26) and the three starting chips. Green doors do not consume this key, so the task is to manage the seven Teeth as each room opens.
  2. Collect (17,24), then lure the released Teeth against the thin wall at (21,20) while taking (22,20). Use the lower-right loop to take (23,24) and return safely to the inner corridor.
  3. Clear the eastern rooms next: (24,12), (23,16), and (24,17). Leave the central Teeth behind its door while visiting the western rooms, so it cannot cut off those return paths.
  4. Collect (9,24) and (10,22). Combine the neighboring upper-left rooms in one visit, taking (8,17) and (9,16); this avoids an extra lure circuit and keeps the route within the 100-second limit.
  5. Visit the inner-left chip at (15,24), release and evade the central Teeth to collect (16,17), then take the last chip at (8,12). Loop back to the socket at (16,13) and enter the exit immediately south at (16,14).
  6. The unmodified level completes at tick 1,784 with seed 0 and stepping 0. The 431 recorded moves are cardinal keyboard commands; there are no mouse or diagonal commands. The encoded TWS has identical complete native and web traces.
