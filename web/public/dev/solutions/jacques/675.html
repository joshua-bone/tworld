675. Saturn

Impossibility argument

Both exits are inaccessible: one is sealed by permanent walls, and a south-force barrier prevents reaching the red key needed for the other. Blocks cannot provide a safe backstop.

244 chips required · 550 second time limit

Open level in TWO

Why completion is impossible

  1. The alternate exit at (5,16) lies in a six-cell component: columns 4–5, rows 15–17. Permanent hidden walls form its boundary, with ordinary walls at (4,18) and (5,18) below. No teleport lies inside, so Chip cannot reach this exit.
  2. The main exit at (0,0) and the only red key at (5,1) lie north of two south-force fields: columns 1–4 and 6–9, rows 4–7. Permanent walls at columns 0, 5 and 10 prevent side entry at these rows. Row 0 offers no entrance from elsewhere. Neither layer contains suction boots, and no teleport reaches this northern region.
  3. Any first approach from below must move north from row 8 into row 7. This manual move sets the moved flag. Before another manual move is allowed, the next even-tick force poll moves Chip south again. A move immediately before a four-tick boundary does not help: forced movement occurs before the flag reset and manual input. A subsequent north move returns only to row 7.
  4. A safe backstop cannot appear in the row 8 cell that Chip just vacated. Row 8, columns 1–9, initially contains water; filling it with blocks produces dirt. These cells contain no force floor, ice, teleport or cloner, so a block cannot move into the cell autonomously from an adjacent row 8 cell. Permanent walls across row 9 prevent entry from the south.
  5. A block entering that row 8 cell from the north would first have to pass through Chip at row 7. An ordinary block collision is fatal. The special temporary squish state applies only to a block leaving a cloner, and no cloner is adjacent to this barrier. A monster in the destination also causes a lethal collision rather than providing a safe backstop.
  6. Chip’s forced movement runs before forced movement of blocks and monsters. Pushing a block north onto the south-force field cannot create a higher starting position: the block becomes sliding, and MS rejects another push parallel or opposite to its sliding direction. The lower block cloner therefore supplies no way to defeat the row 7 obstruction.
  7. Thus every surviving visit to row 7 returns to row 8 before Chip can climb to row 6. Chip cannot reach the red key or pass the red door at (1,0) to the main exit. The other exit is sealed, so no legal MS play can complete the level.
