Findings so far

  1. The alternate exit (5, 16) is in a six-cell component x 4..5, y 15..17. Its complete boundary is permanent hidden walls, with ordinary Walls (4, 18),(5, 18) below. No teleport lies inside. Therefore that exit is unreachable.
  2. The main exit (0, 0) and its red key (5, 1) lie north of south-force fields x 1..4 andx 6..9, y 4..7. Column x 5 and the outer side boundaries are permanent Walls here; row 0 provides no entrance from the rest of the level. There are no suction boots or upper-region teleports.
  3. Any first entrance into this northern region must climb from row 8 through south-force row 7 into row 6. A manual north move from row 8 enters row 7 and sets the moved flag. Before Chip can make another manual move, the next forced-movement poll pushes Chip south back into the cell just vacated on row 8. Manual north can return Chip to row 7 but cannot gain a further row.
  4. This backward push cannot be blocked by a pre-existing block in the vacated cell, because Chip just occupied that cell. No autonomous block or block cloner can insert a block there while Chip is on row 7. A monster occupying that cell causes a lethal collision rather than a safe backstop. There is no side entry at row 7 that bypasses the first north-entry argument.
  5. The lower block cloner cannot launch a block directly into these cells. Ordinary blocks pushed north onto the south-force field are sliding and cannot be pushed again parallel or opposite to that force. They cannot give Chip a new source position above the barrier.
  6. Inductively Chip cannot reach row 6 of either field, hence cannot reach the red key, door (1, 0), or exit (0, 0). Together with the sealed alternate-exit component, this prevents completion.
