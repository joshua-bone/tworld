939. The Nab II

Impossibility argument

The only passages out of the upper area strip the sole fire boots, making return to the exit lethal. Staying inside yields at most 317 of 411 required chips.

411 chips required · 999 second time limit

Open level in TWO

Why completion is impossible

  1. The sole Exit is (0,0). It can only be entered through Socket (1,0): (0,1) is a permanent Wall and the other two sides are board boundaries. Therefore all 411 required chips must be collected before returning to the upper area.
  2. Consider the three Burglar cells (12,20), (12,21), and (12,22). Each has Fire immediately east at (13,20..22). Permanent Walls and Clone Machines leave those three cells as the only passages from the upper starting area and fire-boots room into the rest of the board.
  3. The only Fire Boots in either plane are at (18,21), on the upper-area side of this boundary. Entering any of the three burglars clears all boots. No Fire Boots exist beyond the boundary. Returning east from the burglar strip must enter one of the three Fire cells without immunity, which burns Chip. Moving north or south within the strip does not bypass the Fire.
  4. The barrier cannot be removed: there are no blocks, teleports, or cloner connections anywhere in the level. Clone Machines and ordinary Walls are permanent. Monsters do not extinguish Fire or consume burglars; their ordinary arrival and departure preserve those tiles. Overlapping a moving actor with the player is a losing collision, not a traversable replacement for the strip.
  5. For a conservative upper bound, block only permanent Walls, Clone Machines, and the three one-way burglar boundary cells. Grant unrestricted passage through every other tile, including all fire, dirt, doors, actors, sockets, force floors, ice, and toggles. The start component has 494 cells and contains exactly 317 chips across both original planes. The complete sets are recorded in 939-cut.json; the other 126 chips lie outside.
  6. A play that leaves this component cannot return to its sole exit. A play that remains in the component can collect at most 317 chips, below 411, so its exit socket cannot open. Therefore no play can complete the level.
