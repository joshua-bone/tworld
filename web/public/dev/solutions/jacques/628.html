Winning strategy

  1. Expose and collect the initial flippers at (6,8). Reach the fire boots at (20,0), wait for the Ball to turn away, then leave and reenter to collect the boots.
  2. Collect the upper fire-corridor chips and green key (22,20), then clear the accessible upper and left areas before crossing the burglar (24,12). Collect replacement fire boots (25,12).
  3. Use all nine blocks in the right room to fill the nine Water-over-Chip cells (28,19) through (28,27). After every fill, walk onto the resulting Dirt, leave, and reenter to collect the buried chip before sending the next block along the same column. The block-source chips are collected while pushing.
  4. Collect the lower perimeter and all chips in the two-Bug room. Push the final block (10,28) north into Water (10,27), then clear the Dirt and collect its buried chip. All 99 chips are now collected.
  5. Open the socket at (5,29), leave and reenter for the red key beneath it, open the door (1,27), and enter teleport (6,27) to reach the exit (29,7).
