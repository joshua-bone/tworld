Why completion is impossible

  1. An exhaustive inventory of all 1,024 cells in both layers finds zero actual Exit tiles. Decorative Chip images are different tile types and cannot finish the level.
  2. The native MS completion condition requires Chip to arrive on an actual Exit. No available movement, button, clone, or terrain transformation creates an Exit tile.
  3. Therefore no legal input sequence can complete this unmodified level. This does not depend on random seed or timing.
