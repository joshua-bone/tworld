Why completion is impossible

  1. The 40 required chips are the only 40 chips in the file. They form a snake at x 22..30, y 23..30. Every chip additionally has Water beneath it.
  2. The chip snake is entirely surrounded by top-layer Water. The mainland opening is gravel (20, 27), but its eastern neighbor (21, 27) is Water; it is not a direct entrance onto chip (22, 27). The entire x 21 boundary of the lake, y 22..31, is Water.
  3. There are no flippers or blocks in either layer. The only cloner is a glider cloner, so it cannot create blocks or boots. No teleports exist.
  4. Entering the separating top-layer Water without flippers kills Chip. Only blocks could fill it, and none exist or can be created. Monsters cannot bridge water. Thus no required chip can be collected.
  5. All three exits are under Sockets at (11, 11),(12, 11),(15, 11), so none can be entered with 40 chips outstanding.
