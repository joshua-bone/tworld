732. Bazzar

Impossibility argument

At least 46 red keys are needed for the start gate and nine mandatory chip alcoves, but only 45 red keys exist.

52 chips required · 320 second time limit

Open level in TWO

Why completion is impossible

  1. All 52 chips are mandatory because both entrances to the sole exit are sockets
  2. Nine sealed chip alcoves each require opening five red doors (45 total)
  3. The starting chamber has a different red door as its sole exit, costing at least one more key
  4. Only 45 red keys exist across both layers, so at least 46 are needed but at most 45 can be obtained
  5. The second red door under the starting doorway is not counted in this lower bound
  6. There are no blocks, cloners or teleporters; monsters cannot open red doors or create red keys.
