Why completion is impossible

  1. Initial Chip (21, 8) starts in the upper/right area. There are zero keys of any color in either layer, and no cloners or teleports.
  2. The west-hand lower chamber has its only entrance from the starting side through green door (6, 14). Row 13 consists of permanent Walls from x 0 through 13 except the opening at x 6. Column 13 separates this chamber from the right side through y 31, apart from openings above the chamber. The bottom-left exterior is accessible only through Exit (1, 21).
  3. A permissive flood fill that treats every non-permanent tile as passable except green doors and sockets reaches 582 cells from Chip but not Exit (1, 21); its only green-door frontier is (6, 14). This is a certificate of the stated immutable cut, not a heuristic gameplay search.
  4. Chip starts without a green key and none can be produced. Monsters/blocks cannot enter or remove a green door. Thus the cut cannot open. Independently eight required chips lie under the eight green doors, with 37 required and exactly 37 present.
