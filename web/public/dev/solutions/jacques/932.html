Winning strategy

  1. MS supplies the default player at (0,0). Its first move overwrites the starting chip without counting it toward the quota, leaving 1,028 collectible units from the 1,029 raw chip tiles. The 29 surplus units above the required 999 allow selected chips to remain as permanent monster barriers and protect the final approach to the exit.
  2. Preclear the upper-right approach, then lure five Teeth down the right edge and west along the bottom button row. By tick 1,543 the controller Ball is pinned at (24,31) and five Teeth occupy (25,31) through (29,31). Retained chips directly above them prevent the Teeth from following Chip north. These six actors stay on their buttons for the rest of the replay, preventing repeated activations of six sources. The seventh button at (30,31) controls a Bug cloner and is not pinned.
  3. Clear the outer regions in a planned circuit: bottom, west, top, then the right boundary and southeast pocket. Advance through cleared floor and protected chip landings, checking that each collected group has a safe departure. Retain the right-edge guide chips, the roof above the Teeth queue, two unsafe southeast chips, and the Walker outlet at (9,17). The outer collection is finished by tick 6,735.
  4. Enter the core from its southwest corner and clear paired rows from the bottom upward, using short delays and retreats through cleared floor to avoid monsters. Keep both layers of the three chips at (16,11), (16,12), and (16,13) intact throughout the cleanup. The final compact upper group is cleared while preserving this exit corridor.
  5. At tick 10,531, Chip stands at (16,10) with two required chips remaining. Hold south: the landings at (16,11) and (16,12) satisfy the quota; (16,13) supplies one optional extra chip. Each landing leaves its lower chip layer in place as a monster barrier. Chip enters the Socket-over-Exit at (16,14) at tick 10,544.
  6. The exact TWS completes in both the repository native Tile World 2.3 MS engine and production web MS engine, with matching initial state, all 10,545 tick snapshots, and final result. Seed 0, stepping 4, and all 2,274 recorded commands are cardinal keyboard inputs; no mouse or diagonal commands are used.
