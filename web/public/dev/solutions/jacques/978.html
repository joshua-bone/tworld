Winning strategy

  1. Clear the top-left 2×3 rectangle with S S E N W S E N N W E, holding each direction for four ticks. This opens the Walker source outlet into a growing cleared area instead of leaving a saturated pocket behind mandatory chips.
  2. Clear the adjacent top 2×2 block by alternating visits to its upper pair and lower pair. Both layers of the full 4×3 opening are empty by tick 72.
  3. Sweep the remaining map in 2×2 regions, following pairs of columns from left to right. Sweep alternate column pairs downward and upward. The final pair, columns 30–31, runs upward toward the exit at (31,0).
  4. Finish both chip layers of each active region before advancing. Never collect from a future region. At every completed-region transition, all remaining chips lie in at most one connected component; no scattered groups are left for a dangerous return trip.
  5. Within each small region, choose a safe order of cardinal moves, short delays, and retreats through already-cleared floor. The actual native engine resolves Walker movement and collisions; a seemingly productive pickup is rejected if the following monster phase kills Chip.
  6. The resulting replay collects all 1,940 required chips and enters the socket/exit at (31,0) at tick 9,812, using seed 0 and stepping 0. All 2,067 recorded moves are cardinal keyboard inputs. The original native MS engine and production web engine have identical initial states, all 9,813 tick snapshots, and final results.
